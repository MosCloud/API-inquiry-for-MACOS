# API Inquiry v0.4.4

### App Optimization

- **When a quota runs out, the panel tells you what can still pay**: once either Codex window is drained, the menu bar, the hero and the secondary rows switch to credits together — no more "0% in the bar, 100% in the hero" for the same account. If both the quota and the credits are empty, it still reads `0%`.
- **A drained window no longer disappears**: the credits used to replace the two quota rows. Now `5h` and `Weekly` keep their percentages and the credit balance sits below them, so you can see what ran out and what can still pay for it.
- **Credits are written one way everywhere**: whole units (`4471`, not `4471.36`), while money keeps its decimals (`29.5`). The bar and the panel no longer disagree about the same balance.
- **WorkBuddy shows its next refresh date again**: its plan packages were being read as one-off grants, so the panel never got a refresh date and the plan's credits landed in the granted column. The packages are now counted where they belong, and the trailing cell shows the next refresh date (`12/07`-style), matching WorkBuddy's own screen.

### Known Limitations

- Apple notarization is not enabled yet; the first launch may need approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
- WorkBuddy is still CN-region only, and needs the desktop app installed and signed in.
- The refresh date is shown without a year, as month/day only.
